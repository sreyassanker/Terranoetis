Simulations

Physics-Driven Hazard Modeling

GPU-accelerated mathematical models computed directly over real 3D terrain meshes.

Earthquake Waves

3D elastic wave propagation (leapfrog) & NGA-West2 GMPE ShakeMap PGA/PGV/Sa/MMI intensity.

Hurricane Surge

Parametric wind fields, barometric pressure, wave setup, and coastal inundation.

Wildfire Spread

Rothermel surface fire spread models using fuel models and slope vectors.

Hydrodynamic Flood

Local-inertial Shallow Water Equations (SWE) with ESA WorldCover roughness over real terrain.

Tsunami Propagation

Finite-volume shallow-water equations (MUSCL + minmod + HLL fluxes) over real GEBCO 2020 bathymetry.

Volcanic Eruption

Conservative Rusanov lava shallow-water solver, buoyant plume, and ash advection-diffusion-settling.

Landslide Debris

Depth-averaged debris flow with Voellmy friction law over real Cesium terrain.
